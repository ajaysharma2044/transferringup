The University of Georgia's deadline for spring 2027 transfer entry was July 15. It has already passed. I lead with that because the transfer application timeline for 2026-2027 does not behave like the high school admissions calendar you remember, where everything pointed at one date in January. Transfer season has two separate entry points, deadlines scattered from midsummer to mid-March, and a stack of paper checkpoints in between that kill more applications than weak essays do. I went from a 2.9 to Cornell as a transfer, and honestly the biggest edge I had was a calendar taped above my desk. What follows is that calendar rebuilt for this cycle, with the dates checked against each school's own admissions pages.

## Spring 2027 entry: the small door that closes first

Spring transfer is a niche market, and you should know that before you fall in love with it. Of the eight Ivies, Brown is the only one with a spring option this cycle, and it is restricted to sophomore transfers. Cornell's admissions page says it outright: transfer applications are considered for the fall semester only, and Harvard, Yale, and Princeton admit transfers for fall terms only as well. The realistic spring list is mostly public flagships, and their deadlines arrive far earlier than people expect.

| School | Spring 2027 deadline | Decision timing |
|---|---|---|
| UGA (Athens campus) | July 15, 2026, already passed | Mid to late August 2026 |
| Georgia Tech | September 15, the date it has held in recent cycles | Fall 2026 |
| Brown (sophomore transfers only) | October 1, 2026 | Late November 2026 |
| UW-Madison | October 1, 2026, materials by October 15 | On or before December 31, 2026 |
| Cornell | Not offered | Fall entry only, per the admissions office |

Brown's spring round moves fast once it opens. The mid-term report is due November 3, decisions post in late November, and admitted students get about two weeks to reply, per the transfer calendar Brown publishes. Wisconsin promises an answer by December 31 if you file by October 1. So a spring application is a ten-week sprint from submission to decision.

Spring entry suits a student whose reason for leaving is urgent and whose record is already strong, because there is no time to repair anything before the file gets read. If your last semester went sideways, fall 2027 entry gives you one more full graded term to change the story, and I wrote about how that works in [can you transfer with a low GPA](/blog/can-you-transfer-with-a-low-gpa).

## The transfer application timeline for fall 2027, month by month

Fall entry is the main event. Where a school has not published its 2026-27 date yet, I say so and give you last cycle's date instead of a guess.

### August 2026

The UC application opened August 1. Nothing is due yet, but open the account now, because the coursework and activities sections take longer than any essay. This is also list-building month: for each school, record the deadline, the platform (Common App, Coalition, or the school's own portal), and the required forms. And register carefully, because the fall schedule you pick this month is the last full semester of grades any committee will see. Take courses in your intended major that you can win.

### September 2026

If California is the goal, the Transfer Admission Guarantee window runs September 1 to 30. TAG is a contract: meet the GPA and coursework floors and a participating UC campus guarantees your admission. Berkeley, UCLA, and San Diego do not participate, but a locked seat at Davis or Irvine changes how boldly you can build the rest of the list. September is also when you ask professors for recommendations, while they still remember your spring work and before their November request pile forms. Hand them your why-transfer reasoning and a resume so the letter has texture.

### October 2026

Three things land on October 1: the UC filing window opens, Brown and Wisconsin close their spring rounds, and the 2027-28 FAFSA is scheduled to launch, with the Education Department planning its usual October 1 release. File the FAFSA early even if you doubt you will qualify for much; transfer aid offices process files in the order they complete. By mid-October, confirm every recommender has actually started writing rather than merely agreed to.

### November 2026

The UC deadline is November 30 for all nine campuses. Submit the week before, not the last night. This is also drafting month for the main transfer essay, the one about what you have done where you are and what specifically you are moving toward. Every school reads some version of it, so the base draft has to be strong before you tailor anything.

### December 2026

USC's December 1 deadline covers its portfolio and audition programs: Kaufman for dance, Thornton for music, and the BFA tracks in cinematic and dramatic arts. Wisconsin releases spring decisions by December 31. For everyone else, December is about finishing the term, because fall grades are the newest academic evidence in your file and every conditional offer later will assume you held the line.

### January 2027

The UC Transfer Academic Update is due January 31. You log back in, report your fall grades, and list winter and spring courses. Skipping it can sink an otherwise finished application, so put it on the calendar twice. UGA's fall application opens January 22. January is also supplement season, the why-this-school essays with named professors, named courses, named programs. I collected what works in [transfer essay examples and tips](/blog/transfer-essay-examples-and-tips).

### February 2027

The first fall deadline cluster. February 1 is Michigan and Wisconsin's priority round. February 15 was UNC Chapel Hill and USC last cycle, and I expect the same window this year since neither had posted a 2026-27 date when I checked. You could push back that a date pulled from last cycle might move. It might, by days, which is why your January homework is rebuilding this table from each school's page and treating mine as the scaffold. One filter worth knowing from Michigan's own admissions page: competitive applicants typically show two or more semesters of graded college coursework at the time they apply.

### March 2027

The Ivy month. March 1 belongs to Harvard, Yale, Princeton, Dartmouth, Brown, and Columbia, which takes transfers only through the Coalition application on Scoir. UVA, UGA, and Wisconsin's regular round share the date. March 15 is Cornell and Penn. Cornell gives you until April 15 for remaining materials, but the application itself and the fee are due on the 15th. If Cornell is the target, my full playbook is in [how to transfer to Cornell](/blog/how-to-transfer-to-cornell). Whatever the school, submit three days early. A portal outage on deadline night is your emergency, not theirs.

### April 2027

April looks quiet and decides a lot. Mid-term reports come due, and Brown wants yours by April 1, or April 16 if you are on the quarter system. UC decisions arrive in waves through late April. Wisconsin's regular round posts by April 30. Keep going to class and keep scoring, because your final transcript gets checked against the conditions of every offer before you enroll.

### May 2027

UGA's enrollment deposit is due May 15. Brown and Dartmouth release transfer decisions around mid-May, with roughly two weeks to reply. USC promises either a decision or a request for your spring grades by May 31. Hold on to that USC detail, because it sets up the collision I cover below.

### June and July 2027

June 1 is the UC Statement of Intent to Register deadline. Cornell releases decisions anywhere from late April through June, per its admissions page. USC's grade-dependent decisions roll out from late May to mid-July. Waitlists at the March schools move through this window too. Order final transcripts the day spring grades post; offers stay conditional until the new school has them in hand.

## The checkpoints nobody warns you about

Every rejected-on-a-technicality file I have seen died at one of these, not at the essay.

### The College Report signature

Nearly every selective school wants a College Report, a form an official at your current school completes to confirm your standing and conduct. Not you, an official: someone in the registrar's office, a dean of students, or a designated advisor. Find out this fall which office handles these, because in late February that office is buried, and a two-week turnaround will blow a March 1 deadline. You need one per college you have attended, including a dual-enrollment community college from high school.

### The mid-term report

At Common App schools this one is a self-report form; the help center that runs Common App for transfer states plainly that your college should not fill it out for you. You list current courses with midterm grades or your professors' best estimates. The timing is the trap. A March application gets read while your spring semester is half finished, which makes this form the freshest evidence a committee sees. Ask each professor for a graded midterm or a written estimate in late March, before you need it in the portal.

### Recommenders, asked early

A professor asked in September writes from memory and detail. A professor asked in February writes from your final grade. Same person, very different letter. Ask early, then send one polite nudge two weeks before each deadline, because "invited" and "submitted" are different statuses in every application system.

### FAFSA and the aid forms

File the 2027-28 FAFSA in October and add school codes as your list firms up. The CSS Profile schools, which include the Ivies, want that form too. An aid office cannot build a package from an incomplete file, and a late package can push your decision past another school's deposit date.

### The deposit and waitlist collision

Watch how the dates stack. UGA wants a deposit by May 15. The UCs want your commitment by June 1. Cornell might not answer until June, and USC might not answer until July. So you will probably put a deposit down at a school you are not fully sure about, and that money is the price of a guaranteed seat. Budget for it now and it stings less in May. What you should not do is hold two enrollment deposits into the summer; that violates the enrollment agreement you sign, and it can cost you both offers.
